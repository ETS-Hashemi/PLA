\section{Experimental evaluation}\label{sec:evaluation}

\paragraph{Status.} This section reports four studies, all regenerated by
the committed scripts: a seeded synthetic harness validation
($n{=}2{,}000$), and three \emph{real-data} designs --- the ULB
credit-card fraud data~\cite{dalpozzolo2015} (284{,}807 transactions, 492
frauds, verified against its published invariants) under three random
split seeds, and the official replication data of Bao et
al.~\cite{bao2020} (146{,}045 firm-years, 964 AAER frauds) under two
temporal designs. Every AUC carries a seeded percentile-bootstrap 95\%
confidence interval ($B{=}500$); AUC point estimates are quoted exactly
as generated, while intervals and secondary metrics are displayed at
four decimals. Baselines include two
equal-interpretability competitors --- a depth-4 balanced decision tree
and an EBM~\cite{lou2013,nori2019} --- alongside logistic regression,
untuned gradient boosting, ProbLog executing the same rules, and a pgmpy
naive Bayes. Bao runs approximate the original serial-fraud protocol
(post-first-fraud firm-years dropped from training; counts recorded in
the generated files).

Three findings. First, the accuracy frontier on this dataset is itself
interpretable: EBM tops the table at $0.9815$, just above logistic
regression. Second, an 11-rule, fully hand-traceable \pla{} model sits
$0.013$--$0.019$ below that frontier (static $0.9687$; learned $0.9625$
with near-perfect calibration, ECE $0.0022$) --- confidence intervals of
the frontier models and \pla{} overlap. Across three split seeds the
picture is stable: \pla{} static spans $0.9525$--$0.9687$, learned
$0.9485$--$0.9628$, logistic regression $0.9700$--$0.9796$, EBM
$0.9747$--$0.9815$. Third, this study surfaced a model-class pathology,
preserved pre-fix in the repository's history: without the intercept the
learned variant's AUC was $0.076$ --- inverted --- for the reason given
in Section~\ref{sec:learning}; the intercept restores it. Untuned
default gradient boosting collapses at this class imbalance; we report
defaults by design and note it as a limitation.

\subsection{Real data II: accounting fraud under temporal drift}

Two temporal designs on the Bao et al.\ data (13 discretized ratios;
issuance indicator as context; complete-case). The \emph{strict} design
trains through fiscal 2001 and tests from 2003 (62{,}087 training
firm-years after serial-fraud handling / 58{,}405 test). The
\emph{SOX-boundary} design (Table~\ref{tab:sox}) trains through 2005 ---
spanning the 2003 regime change, so a PostSOX context weight is
identifiable --- with a two-year gap and testing from 2008.

In the strict design, tail rules mined pre-2002 barely rank later frauds:
\pla{} static $0.5285$ [0.5028, 0.5585], learned $0.4905$, no-context
ablation $0.4733$, against $0.6794$ for raw-ratio logistic regression and
$0.666$ for EBM. The context ablation shows a small positive gap
($+0.017$) whose intervals overlap.

\paragraph{The pre-registered verdict on context conditioning.} Even
under the design built to make the context weight identifiable, the
mechanism adds nothing: learned $0.4651$ vs.\ ablation $0.464$, with
essentially identical intervals and calibration. Combined with the null
on credit card ($0.9625$ vs.\ $0.963$) and the within-CI sliver on the
strict design, the conclusion is that context-conditioned rule weights,
\emph{with the coarse single-bit context variables tested here}, show no
reliable benefit --- reported plainly per the kill criterion recorded in
the repository's gap statement before these experiments ran. The
demonstrated value of learning lies in calibration, not in context
adjustment; Section~\ref{sec:discussion} draws the drift lesson.

Deletion metrics in the faithfulness framing of~\cite{jacovi2020}:
comprehensiveness (prediction drop when the trace's top rule's facts are
removed) and sufficiency, against a control that ranks the same fired
rules worst first. The static model's traces out-score the control on
all four datasets (Table~\ref{tab:fidelity}). For the calibrated learned
model on rare-event data the absolute-probability deltas compress to
noise level (e.g.\ $0.0002$ vs.\ $0.0001$ on credit card) --- correctly
ordered but uninformative at this scale; log-odds-unit variants are the
needed refinement (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}).

\subsection{Case study: one real fraud, traced}\label{sec:casestudy}

The generated exhibit (\texttt{results/case\_study\_creditcard.md})
walks the highest-scored true positive in the credit-card test split
through the engine. Eleven rules fire; the noisy-OR fold accumulates
line by line from the strongest pair rule
(V10\_low $\wedge$ V4\_high, empirical precision $0.1949$) to a static
score of $0.4929$ against a base rate of $0.0017$. The deletion
counterfactual quantifies the top factor: removing its two facts drops
the static score from $0.4929$ to $0.2552$ (learned: $0.0189$ to
$0.0069$). This artifact --- weighted domain rules whose provenance is
an auditable training-set measurement, folded by explicit arithmetic,
with a counterfactual attached --- is what none of the baselines
produce, and is the deliverable the audit setting requires.
