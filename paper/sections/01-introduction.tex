\section{Introduction}\label{sec:intro}

In audit, compliance, and clinical settings, a model that cannot show a
person \emph{why} it concluded something is often unusable regardless of
its accuracy. Regulators demand justifications for individual decisions,
auditors must defend every flag they raise, and the case for inherently
interpretable models over post-hoc explanations of black boxes in
high-stakes settings is well established~\cite{rudin2019,dimarino2025}. At the same
time, the systems that offer strong probabilistic semantics for logical
knowledge, probabilistic logic programming and statistical relational
learning (Section~\ref{sec:related}), present two obstacles to exactly
these users: their inference machinery (knowledge compilation, MRF
inference) is opaque to the domain practitioner who must sign off on a
decision, and their rule weights are fixed at inference time, so a rule's
reliability cannot respond to the operational context in which it fires.

\pla{}\footnote{\pla{} abbreviates \emph{Probabilistic Logic Agent},
the project's original name, kept for continuity with the released
package (\texttt{probabilistic-logic-agent}). As
Section~\ref{sec:notprob} makes precise, the values the engine
manipulates are confidences, not probabilities of a distribution over
possible worlds; the name records the lineage, not a semantic claim.}
explores the opposite corner of the design space. It is a
rule-confidence engine simple enough to trace by hand, a few hundred
lines of dependency-free Python, that is honest about \emph{not} being
a probability distribution, and that invests the saved complexity in
three places: context-conditioned rule weights, learnability, and
measured explanation quality. The first of these enters as a
hypothesis, not a claim: the paper's own pre-registered ablations end
up rejecting it for the coarse context features tested, and we keep the
mechanism in the exposition precisely because the negative result is
informative about \emph{what kind} of adaptation drift actually
demands.

\paragraph{Contributions}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{An explicit confidence calculus with verified properties}
  (Section~\ref{sec:calculus}). All operators are named and formally
  defined; the definitions are executable, the test suite extracts and
  runs every formula in the semantics document, and fixpoint
  convergence for the monotone aggregators is proved
  (Proposition~\ref{prop:convergence}) and corroborated by
  property-based tests on 1{,}050 random cyclic rule systems.
\item \textbf{Context-conditioned, learnable rule weights}
  (Section~\ref{sec:learning}). Context variables contribute additive
  log-odds evidence, avoiding the saturation that afflicts
  multiply-and-cap certainty-factor updates; on flat rule sets over
  binary facts the model class collapses to logistic regression over
  rule firings, so maximum-likelihood fitting is convex, trained under
  a backtracking descent guard, and recovers planted ground truth. The
  same reduction shows the mechanism adds interface, not expressivity:
  fixed-weight systems can encode it as context-conjoined rules ---
  and across three real-data designs, pre-registered ablations show no
  practically useful gain on the pre-specified endpoint, a negative
  we report per its kill criterion (Section~\ref{sec:evaluation}).
\item \textbf{Measured explanation fidelity, with its exactness
  boundary drawn honestly}
  (Section~\ref{sec:evaluation}). Rule traces are the engine's primary
  output, and their internal fidelity is quantified rather than
  asserted: rule-level deletion metrics in the comprehensiveness /
  sufficiency family of ERASER~\cite{deyoung2020} (facts untouched,
  avoiding the overlapping-antecedent confound), on probability and
  log-odds scales. In the flat crisp experiments \emph{both} models'
  rankings are provably the exact deletion ranking, so those tables
  are reported as implementation-concordance checks; a chained
  multi-step study, where the local candidate ranking is genuinely
  fallible, then measures it against a brute-force deletion oracle
  and finds it barely above chance --- a measured boundary that
  scopes trace trust to single-fold conclusions and hands chained
  ones to the engine's exported deletion counterfactuals.
\item \textbf{A reproducibility discipline}
  (Section~\ref{sec:implementation}). Every number reported in this
  paper regenerates byte-for-byte from committed scripts; claims about
  competitor capabilities are themselves verified by tests against the
  installed competitor packages; and the supplementary material
  (Table~S1) maps
  each substantive claim to the artifact that verifies it.
\end{enumerate}

Section~\ref{sec:related} positions \pla{} against probabilistic logic
programming, weighted-logic learning, and its own certainty-factor
lineage. Sections~\ref{sec:calculus} and~\ref{sec:learning} present the
calculus and the learning theory. Section~\ref{sec:implementation}
describes the tool. Section~\ref{sec:evaluation} reports the synthetic
harness validation and two real-data studies, including an honest
negative result under temporal drift, and a model-class pathology whose
repair the reduction of Section~\ref{sec:learning} made obvious.
Section~\ref{sec:limitations} lists limitations explicitly.
