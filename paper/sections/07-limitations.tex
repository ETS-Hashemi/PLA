\section{Limitations and threats to validity}\label{sec:limitations}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{No distribution semantics.} \pla{} provides no joint
  measure and no conditioning; correlated evidence is combined under a
  \emph{chosen} independence stance (Section~\ref{sec:notprob}). This is
  a scoping decision, not an oversight, but it bounds the claims the
  system can make.
\item \textbf{Propositional, negation-free language.} The symbolic layer
  accepts definite clauses only; constraint-mode gating cannot block
  contradicted conclusions until negation lands, and is documented as a
  no-op.
\item \textbf{Discretization tax.} Section~\ref{sec:evaluation} shows
  rule-shaped models paying a visible accuracy cost on continuous
  signal; rule mining and threshold selection are first-order levers we
  have not yet tuned.
\item \textbf{Simplified real-data protocols.} The credit-card study
  uses random splits (the dataset spans two days; temporal structure is
  limited), and the accounting-fraud study still simplifies the original
  protocol of~\cite{bao2020}: complete-case deletion of the 13.4\% of
  firm-years with missing ratios, an approximation of their serial-fraud
  handling (post-first-fraud firm-years dropped from training only), and
  a much coarser rule vocabulary than their 28-feature ensembles. Its
  negative results bind these protocols, not the hypothesis space.
  Baselines run library defaults by design, with two stated
  exceptions: a class-weighted
  variant added for gradient boosting after its default collapse, and
  RuleFit's declared rule budget (\texttt{max\_rules}=30, no linear
  terms) chosen for complexity parity rather than accuracy
  (Section~\ref{sec:evaluation}); nothing else is tuned, which may
  understate every baseline.
\item \textbf{What fidelity metrics can and cannot show.} The trace is
  generated from the same computation it explains, so deletion metrics
  here validate \emph{internal consistency}, that the exported
  ranking matches what actually moves the score, rather than any
  external notion of explanation quality. In the flat crisp
  experiments that consistency is exact by algebra for \emph{both}
  models --- the learned per-rule contribution is $z_r$ by
  construction, and Eq.~\ref{eq:noisyor-deletion} fixes the static
  deletion ranking --- so those tables are implementation-concordance
  checks, not tests of a fallible ranking; the chained study is the
  only setting reported here where the trace ranking can genuinely
  fail, and its oracle-agreement rate is the honest headline.
  Absolute-probability deltas additionally
  compress for calibrated rare-event models
  (Section~\ref{sec:evaluation}); the rule-level and log-odds variants
  reported there are the appropriate instruments.
\item \textbf{Interpretability scope.} The credit-card features are
  anonymized principal components, so that study demonstrates
  computational traceability, not domain interpretability; the
  accounting study supplies domain-meaningful ratio vocabulary but weak
  discrimination under drift. Neither dataset alone carries the full
  interpretability claim (Section~\ref{sec:evaluation}).
\item \textbf{Statistical scope.} Ten random splits on credit card
  bound split variability but are not a variance study; the temporal
  designs are single splits by construction. Difference claims rest on
  paired-bootstrap intervals over the shared test set, which capture
  resampling uncertainty, not the uncertainty across alternative
  histories of the data-generating process.
\item \textbf{No human-subject evidence.} The usability and
  trace-comprehension claims that motivate the design await an
  ethics-approved study with auditors or students; explanation fidelity
  as measured here is a necessary, not sufficient, condition for human
  utility.
\end{itemize}
