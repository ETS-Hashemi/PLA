\section{Limitations and threats to validity}\label{sec:limitations}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{No distribution semantics.} \pla{} provides no joint
  measure and no conditioning; correlated evidence is combined under a
  \emph{chosen} independence stance (Section~\ref{sec:notprob}). This is
  a scoping decision, not an oversight, but it bounds the claims the
  system can make.
\item \textbf{Propositional, negation-free language.} The symbolic layer
  accepts definite clauses only; constraint-mode gating cannot block
  contradicted conclusions until negation lands, and is documented as a
  no-op.
\item \textbf{Discretization tax.} Section~\ref{sec:evaluation} shows
  rule-shaped models paying a visible accuracy cost on continuous
  signal; rule mining and threshold selection are first-order levers we
  have not yet tuned.
\item \textbf{Simplified real-data protocols.} The credit-card study
  uses a random split (the dataset spans two days; temporal structure is
  limited), and the accounting-fraud study simplifies the original
  protocol of~\cite{bao2020}: complete-case deletion of the 13.4\% of
  firm-years with missing ratios, no serial-fraud handling, and a much
  coarser rule vocabulary. Its negative result binds this protocol, not
  the hypothesis space. Baselines run untuned defaults by design, which
  flatters no one but understates gradient boosting in particular.
\item \textbf{Scale-dependent fidelity metrics.} Deletion metrics in
  absolute probability units compress toward zero for calibrated
  rare-event models (Section~\ref{sec:evaluation}); log-odds-scale
  variants are future work.
\item \textbf{No human-subject evidence.} The usability and
  trace-comprehension claims that motivate the design await an
  ethics-approved study with auditors or students; explanation fidelity
  as measured here is a necessary, not sufficient, condition for human
  utility.
\end{itemize}
