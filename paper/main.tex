\title{\pla: A Transparent, Learnable Rule-Confidence Engine
with Measured Trace Fidelity}

\author[ets]{Seyed Masoud Hashemi Ahmadi\corref{cor1}}
\ead{Seyed-Masoud.Hashemi-Ahmadi.1@ens.etsmtl.ca}
\author[ets]{Julio Montecinos}

\address[ets]{\'Ecole de technologie sup\'erieure (\'ETS), Montr\'eal, Canada}
\cortext[cor1]{Corresponding author.}

\begin{abstract}
We present \pla{}, a rule-confidence model for domains where inherent
interpretability is a requirement rather than a preference. \pla{}
descends openly from MYCIN-style certainty factors: rules carry
confidences, conjunction is the G\"odel t-norm, and co-supporting
rules combine through a named, per-knowledge-base aggregation operator
(noisy-OR by default, provably MYCIN's parallel-combination rule).
Three properties distinguish it from its lineage and from modern
statistical-relational systems: (i)~rule confidences are
\emph{context-conditioned}, through a log-odds mode whose additive
evidence weights never saturate; (ii)~rule and context weights are
\emph{learnable} --- on flat rule sets over binary facts the log-odds
path reduces exactly to logistic regression over rule firings, so
maximum-likelihood fitting is convex; and (iii)~explanations are
\emph{traces whose fidelity is measured}, by rule-level deletion
against reversed- and random-ranking controls. Convergence of the
monotone aggregators is proved, the engine is a few hundred lines of
dependency-free Python, and every reported number regenerates from
committed scripts with bootstrap confidence intervals. On the ULB
credit-card data an 11-rule model averages AUC $0.966$ across ten
random splits, $0.002$--$0.034$ per split below the interpretable
accuracy frontier led by EBM, and its learned weights beat the
constant-prevalence baseline on log-loss. On accounting-fraud data
under two temporal designs, pre-registered ablations backed by
paired-bootstrap difference intervals find no practically useful
improvement from context conditioning on the pre-specified AUC
endpoint; a consistent post-hoc average-precision gain on the
credit-card data awaits confirmation, and we report the negative as
such. Learning buys calibration at a measured early-ranking cost; the
deliverable is the counterfactual-bearing trace.
\end{abstract}
